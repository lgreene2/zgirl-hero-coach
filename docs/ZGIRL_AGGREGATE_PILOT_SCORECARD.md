# Z-Girl Hero Within — Aggregate Pilot Scorecard

Purpose: give institutions and Z-Girl a decision-useful view of implementation quality and participant experience **without using private reflection text as routine reporting data**.

## Scorecard rules

1. Report aggregate or implementation-level information only.
2. Do not paste private participant reflection text into this scorecard.
3. Do not add diagnoses, counseling histories, medical details, safeguarding narratives, or unnecessary personally identifying information.
4. Do not convert the scorecard into a clinical, behavioral, disciplinary, or eligibility assessment.
5. Suppress or combine small-cell results when needed to avoid making individual participants reasonably identifiable.
6. Use only measures approved for the pilot or license scope.
7. Interpret results as implementation evidence—not proof that Z-Girl caused a particular individual outcome.
