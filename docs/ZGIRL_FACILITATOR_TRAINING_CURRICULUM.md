# Z-Girl Facilitator Training Curriculum

Version: 1.0

## Program objective

Prepare candidates to facilitate approved Z-Girl experiences consistently, safely, accessibly, and within role. Completion of training alone does not confer authorization; candidates must also pass the knowledge assessment, practicum, conduct acknowledgment, and authorization review.

## Delivery architecture

Core Facilitator Pathway:
- 6 instructional hours total;
- live, blended, or approved self-paced format;
- 8 modules;
- scenario practice embedded throughout;
- required knowledge assessment;
- 45–60 minute practicum/simulation;
- local safeguarding/emergency orientation;
- authorization decision.

Recommended cohort size:
- 8–24 for live training;
- smaller groups for practicum and calibration.

## Module 1 — What Z-Girl Is and Is Not

Recommended time: 35 minutes

Objectives:
- explain the purpose of Z-Girl;
- describe the Hero Within / Hero Move reflection flow;
- distinguish reflection from counseling, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, clergy/spiritual direction, sports medicine, or crisis intervention;
- state the participant-ownership principle.

Content:
- Z-Girl mission and intended use;
- reflection flow;
- boundaries and non-claims;
- institutional pilot/licensing context;
- examples of correct and incorrect positioning.

Practice:
- 60-second explanation of Z-Girl to a parent, student, athlete, or administrator.

Evidence:
- trainer observation + knowledge-check items.

## Module 2 — Participant Choice, Agency & Trusted-Person Pathways

Recommended time: 40 minutes

Objectives:
- preserve pause, skip, stop, and trusted-person choices;
- avoid coercive disclosure;
- respond appropriately to silence, reluctance, or emotional discomfort;
- distinguish encouragement from pressure.

Content:
- participant agency;
- non-shaming language;
- optional sharing;
- trusted-adult connection;
- group facilitation risks.

Practice scenarios:
- participant refuses to answer;
- peer pressures another participant to share;
- coach/teacher asks facilitator what a participant wrote.

Critical rule:
- private reflection disclosure is never a condition of routine participation.

## Module 3 — Privacy, Data Minimization & Institutional Reporting

Recommended time: 50 minutes

Objectives:
- distinguish private reflection content from implementation data;
- use approved aggregate measures;
- avoid unnecessary personal-data collection;
- explain privacy boundaries accurately.

Content:
- private reflection ownership;
- routine reporting exclusions;
- aggregate/de-identified evidence;
- data-minimization examples;
- appropriate limits of confidentiality;
- approved implementation records.

Practice:
- sort sample data fields into: permitted routine reporting / restricted / not needed.

Critical rule:
- no participant-identifiable private reflection text in routine institutional reporting.

## Module 4 — Safeguarding, Distress & Escalation

Recommended time: 55 minutes

Objectives:
- recognize when a situation exits the standard reflection workflow;
- follow local safeguarding/emergency rules;
- avoid investigating or diagnosing;
- avoid inappropriate secrecy promises;
- know when to stop the session and escalate.

Content:
- scope boundaries;
- local institutional responsibility;
- mandated-reporting/emergency process orientation;
- distress versus crisis;
- documentation boundary;
- appropriate handoff.

Practice:
- scenario triage using local escalation flow.

Critical rule:
- Z-Girl never substitutes for qualified emergency, safeguarding, clinical, medical, pastoral, or sports-medicine support.

## Module 5 — Accessibility & Inclusive Facilitation

Recommended time: 45 minutes

Objectives:
- provide approved access choices;
- adapt pace/modality without changing core boundaries;
- anticipate barriers;
- preserve dignity and participation equivalence;
- coordinate accommodations with institutional owners.

Content:
- plain language;
- reading/listening/response choices;
- sensory load and pacing;
- assistive support;
- accessibility without diagnosis collection;
- inclusive group norms.

Practice:
- redesign a standard session for three different access needs without requiring diagnosis disclosure.

## Module 6 — Profile Fidelity: EDU, Faith & Values, Athlete

Recommended time: 45 minutes

Objectives:
- identify approved profile/version;
- preserve core standards while using setting-specific language;
- avoid unapproved theological, clinical, disciplinary, or performance claims;
- route customization requests through governance.

Content:
- core versus profile-specific content;
- versioning;
- faith-profile boundaries;
- athlete-profile boundaries;
- school/youth-program boundaries;
- approved customization workflow.

Practice:
- determine whether requested facilitator changes are permitted, require approval, or are prohibited.

## Module 7 — Session Delivery, Family Communication & Aggregate Evidence

Recommended time: 50 minutes

Objectives:
- open and close sessions consistently;
- communicate purpose/privacy/support boundaries;
- use approved family-facing language;
- collect implementation evidence without surveillance;
- support closeout decisions.

Content:
- session opening checklist;
- facilitation flow;
- Hero Move / next-step closure;
- family communication;
- scorecard measures;
- implementation barriers and quality notes;
- pilot closeout.

Practice:
- facilitate a 10-minute micro-session;
- complete a mock aggregate scorecard from non-private data.

## Module 8 — Authorization, Conduct, Renewal & Practicum Preparation

Recommended time: 40 minutes

Objectives:
- accurately describe authorization levels;
- understand critical-fail behaviors;
- understand remediation, suspension, revocation, and appeal;
- understand 12-month renewal requirements;
- prepare for practicum.

Content:
- Level 0 through Level 3;
- authorization scope;
- credential IDs/status;
- version currency;
- conduct standards;
- renewal and lapse;
- trainer rights versus facilitator rights.

Practice:
- candidate self-assessment against competency framework.

## Knowledge assessment

Required after core instruction.

Initial standard:
- 85% overall;
- 100% on designated critical privacy/safety items.

Candidates who do not meet the standard enter remediation and reassessment; they do not receive authorization by attendance.

## Practicum

Format:
- 45–60 minute observed simulation or approved live segment;
- candidate must demonstrate opening, privacy/choice boundary, facilitation, response to a challenge scenario, accessibility response, and closure.

Pass standard:
- average 2.5/3 or higher;
- no critical domain below Competent (2);
- no critical-fail behavior.

## Local institutional orientation

Before independent delivery, the institution must provide current information on:
- emergency contacts/process;
- safeguarding/mandated-reporting process;
- consent/permission requirements;
- accessibility/accommodation workflow;
- local supervision/contact person;
- approved implementation schedule/profile.

Z-Girl training does not replace this local orientation.

## Lead Facilitator development

Additional evidence after Level 1:
- two completed implementation cycles or approved equivalent;
- quality review of aggregate evidence;
- observed lead session;
- coaching/calibration exercise;
- current authorization and no unresolved critical issue.

## Institutional Trainer curriculum

Additional 5 instructional hours plus teach-back.

### Trainer Module T1 — Adult learning & trainer role
- training adults without lowering standards;
- distinguishing teaching from authorization decisions.

### T2 — Teaching privacy/safety rationale
- explain why boundaries exist;
- correct common misconceptions.

### T3 — Observation, scoring & calibration
- use the practicum rubric;
- avoid halo effects and inconsistent scoring;
- calibrate on sample performances.

### T4 — Coaching & remediation
- give actionable feedback;
- document remediation;
- identify critical issues requiring escalation.

### T5 — Credential administration & version control
- verify active materials;
- maintain candidate evidence;
- protect credential registry data;
- apply renewal/lapse rules.

### T6 — Teach-back demonstration
- deliver a 30-minute assigned training segment;
- score a mock practicum;
- conduct a remediation conversation.
