## 2. Pilot Purpose

The purpose of the pilot is to evaluate the fit, usability, implementation quality, accessibility, participant experience, and organizational value of the **Z-Girl Hero Within Reflection System** in a controlled implementation setting.

The pilot is intended to support reflection, self-awareness, confidence, character development, trusted-adult connection, and achievable next-step planning. Z-Girl is not therapy, medical care, diagnosis, treatment, emergency support, or a substitute for qualified professional services.

## 5. Privacy and Reflection Boundary

### 5.1 Participant Reflection Ownership

The Z-Girl implementation model is designed so that private participant reflection content is **not** institutional reporting data.

Customer will not require Provider to create or provide a dashboard of private student, athlete, youth, or participant reflections.

### 5.2 No Routine Disclosure of Private Reflections

Unless separately required by applicable law or an approved safety process outside the ordinary Z-Girl reporting workflow, Provider will not intentionally disclose private participant reflection text to Customer as part of pilot reporting.

### 5.3 Aggregate Measures

Customer may receive approved de-identified or aggregate implementation measures specified in this Agreement, such as:

- sessions offered;
- participation;
- completion;
- usefulness;
- confidence / self-efficacy proxy;
- return / reuse;
- identified Hero Move / action rate;
- accessibility experience;
- safety / respect experience; and
- facilitator implementation quality.

### 5.4 Customer Records

Customer remains responsible for determining whether information it provides to Provider is subject to education-record, child-privacy, health-privacy, employment, safeguarding, or other legal restrictions. Customer will not provide Provider with education records, medical records, counseling records, diagnoses, safeguarding case files, or other sensitive records unless expressly required by an approved written scope and legally authorized.

### 5.5 Children’s Privacy Review

If the implementation involves children under 13 or collection of personal information online from children, the Parties will complete an age, consent, data-flow, privacy-notice, retention, and deletion review before launch. The Parties will not assume that school participation alone resolves all applicable children’s privacy requirements.

## 6. Safeguarding and Real-World Support

### 6.1 Customer Responsibility

Customer remains responsible for its own:

- safeguarding policies;
- mandatory-reporting obligations;
- emergency-response procedures;
- crisis protocols;
- supervision requirements;
- staffing decisions; and
- access to qualified professional support.

### 6.2 Z-Girl Role

Z-Girl does not replace counselors, school psychologists, clinicians, clergy acting within professional boundaries, coaches, parents / caregivers, emergency services, or other qualified real-world support.

### 6.3 Facilitator Boundary

Facilitators and coaches will not use Z-Girl to diagnose participants, pressure disclosure, conduct interrogations, or require a participant to reveal a private reflection as a condition of participation.

---

## 7. Accessibility and Participant Choice

Customer and Provider will identify known accessibility needs during readiness review.

The implementation should preserve reasonable participant choice, including the ability to:

- pause;
- skip a prompt;
- stop the experience;
- use approved non-AI reflection pathways where available; and
- seek a trusted person or real-world support.

Customer is responsible for accommodations or services it is legally required to provide unless a specific Provider responsibility is stated in this Agreement.

---

## 8. Customer Responsibilities

Customer will:

1. designate an implementation owner;
2. provide accurate information about the setting, audience, schedule, and relevant operational requirements;
3. ensure approved staff / facilitators complete orientation before leading the pilot;
4. maintain appropriate adult supervision and safeguarding processes;
5. distribute required family / participant communications and obtain any consents or permissions Customer is responsible for obtaining;
6. avoid collecting or redistributing private reflections outside the approved workflow;
7. use Z-Girl materials only for the approved pilot audience and term;
8. protect Provider materials from unauthorized copying, resale, publication, or sublicensing;
9. notify Provider of material implementation issues; and
10. participate in the pilot closeout review.

---

## 9. Provider Responsibilities

Provider will:

1. provide the approved Z-Girl pilot resources;
2. provide the orientation and implementation support stated in this Agreement;
3. maintain the stated privacy and reporting boundaries;
4. use reasonable safeguards for information Provider intentionally receives;
5. avoid representing Z-Girl as clinical treatment or emergency support;
6. communicate material changes to the approved pilot profile; and
7. provide the agreed pilot closeout or findings summary.

---

## 10. Data Minimization

The Parties will use a **minimum-necessary data approach** for the pilot.

Unless expressly added by written amendment, the pilot does not require Customer to transfer:

- student education records;
- medical or mental-health records;
- diagnosis information;
- counseling notes;
- disciplinary case files;
- safeguarding case files;
- government identifiers; or
- private participant journal text.

Any proposed expansion of data collection must be reviewed before implementation.

### 11.2 Limited Pilot License

### 11.3 Prohibited Uses

### 11.4 Customer Materials

Customer retains ownership of its pre-existing names, logos, policies, curriculum, faith materials, team materials, and other content it provides. Any co-developed or derivative material should be addressed in a written addendum before commercial reuse.
