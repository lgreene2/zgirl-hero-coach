# Z-Girl Renewal & Reauthorization Standard

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Maintain current facilitator competence, conduct, version alignment, and institutional quality after initial authorization.

## Standard authorization term

Default term: 12 months from issue date unless the credential specifies a shorter period.

Recommended renewal window:
- opens 60 days before expiration;
- renewal decision completed before expiration where possible;
- credential becomes Lapsed if renewal is not completed by expiration unless an approved extension applies.

## Renewal requirements — Level 1 Authorized Facilitator

Required:
1. active or recently expired credential eligible for renewal;
2. current version/policy acknowledgment;
3. completion of required refresher module(s);
4. renewal knowledge check;
5. evidence of implementation or approved practice within the prior term;
6. no unresolved critical conduct/privacy/safety issue;
7. applicable institutional scope/license confirmation.

Recommended annual renewal knowledge standard:
- 10–15 questions focused on changed standards and critical boundaries;
- 85% overall;
- 100% on critical items.

Observation:
- at least one observation every 24 months for consistently active facilitators;
- annual observation when conditions, remediation history, new profile scope, or institutional agreement requires it.

## Renewal requirements — Level 2 Authorized Lead Facilitator

In addition to Level 1:
- evidence of leading or supporting implementation quality during the term;
- current aggregate quality/closeout evidence appropriate to role;
- calibration/refresher on coaching and role boundaries;
- no unresolved credentialing overreach.

## Renewal requirements — Level 3 Institutional Trainer

In addition to Level 2:
- active institutional trainer license;
- trainer refresher;
- annual scoring-calibration exercise;
- evidence of training activity or approved trainer practicum;
- review of candidate outcomes/remediation patterns without private participant reflection data;
- current trainer agreement and version-control acknowledgment.

## Renewal outcomes

- Renew — Active
- Renew — Active with Conditions
- Short-Term Renewal Pending Evidence
- Remediation Required
- Lapse
- Suspend
- Revoke

## Lapsed credentials

A lapsed holder may not represent the authorization as active.

### 0–90 days lapsed

Credential authority may permit expedited reactivation if:
- no unresolved issue exists;
- current refresher/knowledge requirements are completed;
- institutional scope remains valid.

### 91–365 days lapsed

Typically requires:
- current core refresher;
- full knowledge assessment or approved equivalent;
- targeted observation/practicum;
- scope review.

### More than 365 days lapsed

Treat as reauthorization unless credential authority approves a documented alternative.

## Change-triggered reauthorization

A new full term need not wait for the annual cycle when there is a material change in:
- core safety/privacy policy;
- authorization model;
- role scope;
- specialized profile requirements;
- significant platform workflow affecting facilitation;
- applicable institutional trainer license.

Credential authority may require a targeted update, knowledge check, or reassessment.

## New profile endorsements

An Authorized Facilitator should not automatically assume authorization for every specialized profile.

Where required, add profile scope after:
- profile orientation;
- relevant knowledge/scenario check;
- institutional approval;
- credential record update.

Examples:
- EDU
- Faith & Values
- Athlete
- approved specialized accessibility/content profile

## Continuing development record

Recommended fields:
- credential ID;
- current tier;
- renewal term;
- refresher modules;
- assessments;
- observations;
- profile updates;
- remediation closed/open;
- version acknowledgments;
- status decision.

Do not use participant private reflection text as routine renewal evidence.

## Expiration notices

Recommended notices:
- 60 days before expiration;
- 30 days before expiration;
- 7 days before expiration;
- expiration/lapse notice.

Institutional administrators should receive only the status information needed to manage authorized delivery.

## Renewal integrity

No institution or trainer may:
- extend credential terms indefinitely without the governed process;
- waive critical privacy/safety standards;
- represent a lapsed credential as active;
- substitute institutional employment performance reviews for required Z-Girl renewal evidence without approval.

## Future certification boundary

If Z-Girl later develops a formal certification program, these reauthorization standards may become part of that architecture, but v2.7 does not make a third-party-accreditation or professional-certification claim.
