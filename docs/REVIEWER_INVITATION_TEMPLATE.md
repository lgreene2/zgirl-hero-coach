# Z-Girl Native-Language Reviewer Invitation

## Email subject

Invitation to Review the Z-Girl [Language] Audio Journey

## Invitation message

Hello [Reviewer Name],

I am inviting you to serve as the native-language reviewer for the Z-Girl Hero Within 7-Day Audio Journey in [Language / Region].

Z-Girl is a safety-first reflection system that helps youth and adults slow down difficult moments, recognize their strengths, and choose one achievable next step. Before the translated studio recordings can be considered for public release, each language must be reviewed by a qualified native or professional-level speaker.

Your assignment is limited to [Language / Locale]. You will review seven short sessions in both voice-only and subtle calm-background versions and compare them with the approved English source. The review covers meaning, naturalness, pronunciation, tone, pacing, safety language, and transcript accuracy.

The protected workspace saves your draft only in your browser. It does not automatically publish your review or authorize a public release. You will export the final review record when your checks are complete.

Reviewer portal:
[https://zgirlinitiative.org/review]

For security, your assigned access code will be sent separately. Please do not forward the portal invitation, share the code, or upload candidate recordings or review materials to a public location.

Please reply to confirm that:

1. You are fluent at the native or professional level in [Language / Region].
2. You can evaluate wording appropriate for youth and general audiences.
3. You can keep the candidate recordings and access credentials confidential.
4. You expect to complete the review by [Target Date].

Thank you for helping ensure that Z-Girl sounds natural, respectful, safe, and culturally appropriate in [Language].

Sincerely,

Lyndon Greene
Z-Girl Product Owner
People United Foundation / The 4 Lessons Ecosystem
