### I acknowledge and agree that:

1. Z-Girl is a guided reflection and encouragement system and is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, medical care, crisis intervention, clergy/spiritual direction, sports medicine, or emergency service.
2. I will not represent Z-Girl authorization as professional licensure, academic accreditation, government certification, or clinical qualification.
3. I will preserve participant pause, skip, stop, and trusted-person pathways.
4. I will not require a participant to disclose private reflection content as a routine condition of participation.
5. I will not place participant-identifiable private reflection text in routine institutional reports, scorecards, credential files, or training examples.
6. I will follow the institution's applicable safeguarding, emergency, mandated-reporting, consent/permission, accessibility, and records processes.
7. I will not promise secrecy that conflicts with law or institutional duty.
8. I will not diagnose, clinically score, investigate beyond role, or use Z-Girl as a substitute for qualified support.
9. I will use only current approved Z-Girl materials and profile versions within my authorization scope.
10. I will route material content changes through the approved governance process rather than silently modifying privacy/safety boundaries.
11. I will accurately represent my credential status, tier, scope, and expiration.
12. I will not train or credential others unless I hold the applicable Institutional Trainer Authorization and operate within an active trainer license.
13. I understand that authorization may be conditioned, suspended, lapsed, or revoked under the current policy.
14. I will complete required renewal/version updates to maintain active status.
15. I will protect Z-Girl intellectual property, assessment integrity, and confidential trainer-only materials.
