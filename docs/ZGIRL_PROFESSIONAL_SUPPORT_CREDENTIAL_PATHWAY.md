# Z-Girl Professional Support Credential Pathway

Status: IMPLEMENTATION CANDIDATE — public credential activation and paid launch remain owner-gated.

## Purpose
Extend the existing Z-Girl facilitator authorization, credential registry, verification, renewal, and Train-the-Trainer system for qualified professional-support users without creating a parallel credential system or representing Z-Girl authorization as professional licensure.

## Credential architecture

### Level 1 — Z-Girl Authorized Facilitator
Existing governed program credential for approved facilitation.

### Level 2 — Z-Girl Authorized Lead Facilitator
Existing governed program credential for approved lead facilitation.

### Level 3 — Z-Girl Institutional Trainer — Authorized
Existing governed authorization for approved institutional training capacity.

### Specialization — Z-Girl Professional Support Certified User
Role specialization for an independently qualified professional such as a licensed counselor, therapist, social worker, school counselor, psychologist, or other approved professional-support provider.

This designation verifies Z-Girl product competency only. It does not grant, replace, expand, or validate professional licensure, clinical scope, academic accreditation, government certification, or authority to practice.

### Organization designation — Z-Girl Professional Support Organization
Optional governed designation for an approved counseling practice, school counseling department, youth agency, university, telehealth organization, or other qualified institution implementing Z-Girl under written privacy, safeguarding, credential, and operational requirements.

## Two-layer verification
Professional-support profiles must keep these records separate:

1. Professional qualification
   - profession/title
   - licensing/credential authority
   - jurisdiction
   - license/credential identifier where appropriate for verification
   - independently verified status
   - verification date and source
   - expiration where applicable

2. Z-Girl program authorization
   - Z-Girl credential ID
   - specialization
   - issue date
   - expiration/renewal date
   - current status
   - permitted Z-Girl capabilities

A Z-Girl credential page must never imply that Z-Girl issued or guarantees the external professional license.

## Required learning sequence
1. Z-Girl human-first doctrine and role boundaries
2. Participant-controlled reflection and handoff
3. Privacy, minimum-necessary sharing, and clinical-record separation
4. Youth, guardian, institutional, and safeguarding boundaries
5. AI limitations, transparency, bias awareness, and appropriate reliance
6. Crisis and emergency escalation boundaries
7. Receiving participant-generated conversation briefs
8. Professional documentation boundary: Z-Girl reflection is not automatically a clinical record
9. Consent withdrawal and participant control
10. Scenario practicum and competency assessment

Training completion alone does not create authorization.

## Competency gates
Candidate must satisfy all applicable gates before specialization issuance:
- base Z-Girl training prerequisites;
- identity verification;
- external professional qualification verification when the specialization requires it;
- knowledge assessment;
- scenario/practicum assessment;
- privacy and safeguarding attestation;
- scope-of-use agreement;
- credential operations approval.

## Product handoff model
PARTICIPANT -> PRIVATE REFLECTION -> PREPARE -> CHOOSE WHAT TO SHARE -> CHOOSE SUPPORT PERSON -> CONSENT -> MINIMUM-NECESSARY HANDOFF -> HUMAN PROFESSIONAL

Z-Girl does not autonomously appoint a therapist, diagnose, prescribe treatment, replace emergency services, or silently transfer private reflection content.

## Professional workspace
Eligible credentialed users may receive a governed workspace containing:
- participant-initiated handoff inbox;
- consent and sharing-scope indicator;
- conversation brief viewer;
- role-appropriate response preparation tools;
- resource and escalation references;
- credential status;
- training/renewal status;
- organization assignment where applicable.

Do not expose a participant's private journal/reflection history merely because the professional is credentialed.

## Institutional model
An institution can combine:
- licensed/qualified professionals acting within their independent professional authority;
- Z-Girl Authorized Facilitators acting only within Z-Girl program authority;
- Z-Girl Lead Facilitators;
- approved Institutional Trainers;
- governed professional-support specializations.

Role permissions must remain explicit and non-interchangeable.

## Commercial architecture
Permitted product categories, subject to final commercial approval:
- professional-support training;
- competency assessment;
- credential issuance/renewal administration;
- professional workspace subscription;
- institutional implementation/license;
- continuing Z-Girl education;
- Train-the-Trainer expansion.

Clinical services and professional fees belong to the independent provider/entity and must not be represented as Z-Girl clinical services unless a separately governed and legally approved service is created.

## Renewal
Professional-support specialization renewal should require:
- active base Z-Girl authorization where required;
- current external professional qualification where required;
- policy/update learning;
- periodic scenario calibration;
- safeguarding/privacy re-attestation;
- remediation when triggered;
- credential registry update.

## Public language
Approved concept language:
- "Z-Girl Professional Support Certified User"
- "Verified professional qualification + Z-Girl program competency"
- "Z-Girl authorization is a program credential, not professional licensure."

Avoid:
- "Z-Girl licensed therapist"
- "Z-Girl certified counselor" when it could imply professional certification
- "Z-Girl clinical certification"
- any claim that Z-Girl grants authority to diagnose or treat.

## Release gates
Before public activation:
- legal/compliance review of credential naming and claims;
- privacy/data-flow QA;
- safeguarding and crisis-flow QA;
- external-license verification workflow QA;
- credential registry compatibility QA;
- professional workspace access-control QA;
- owner approval for public credential activation;
- owner approval for pricing/paid launch.
