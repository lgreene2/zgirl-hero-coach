# Z-Girl v3.15 — 30-Day Hero Within Membership Foundation

Status: preview / product-owner review only

## Product role

This build converts the proven 7-Day Hero Within method into the first subscription-ready 30-day content library without weakening Z-Girl's privacy, safety, accessibility, voice, or IP governance boundaries.

The 30-Day Hero Within Journey is designed to become the recurring-revenue bridge between the free 7-Day Journey and future premium themed journeys, family editions, EDU facilitator audio, and monthly Hero Within membership releases.

## Audience

- youth and adults using Z-Girl independently;
- families using the same reflection language together;
- future premium subscribers;
- future institution-licensed participants where local policy and implementation gates permit use.

## Learner experience

Thirty guided sessions are organized into five phases:

1. Make Space — Days 1–7
2. Strength & Support — Days 8–14
3. Hero Moves & Boundaries — Days 15–21
4. Reflect Forward — Days 22–28
5. Integration — Days 29–30

Each day keeps the same reusable learning structure:

- permission and safety;
- focus;
- one reflection prompt;
- strength recognition;
- one achievable Hero Move;
- affirmation;
- reflect-forward close.

## Privacy boundary

The preview is local-first and account-free.

- entries remain in browser local storage on the current device;
- no raw reflection text is sent to institutional dashboards;
- no microphone capture is used;
- progress may be downloaded, printed, or cleared by the participant;
- future authenticated sync must be a separate knowingly chosen capability rather than a silent default.

## Audio governance boundary

The 30-day route is intentionally script-first during this release candidate.

The live Coach's approved v3.14.1 natural AI voice approval is not treated as automatic approval of a 30-day studio master. No provisional audio is presented to a listener as final mastered audio.

Before 30-day audio unlocks, require the exact master to pass:

- approved English script version;
- approved Z-Girl narrator or voice-model rights;
- voice performance review against the Hero Within audio standard;
- exact transcript/audio match;
- voice-only accessible mix;
- music-backed mix only where music rights are documented;
- mastering and device playback QA;
- no autoplay;
- final human listening approval;
- IP registry and authorized-distribution record.

## Commercialization boundary

This branch does not activate billing, create a subscription, charge a user, or claim that paid membership is live.

Recommended commercialization sequence after product-owner approval:

1. approve the 30-day content architecture;
2. produce and approve the English audio master;
3. define entitlement and account-sync boundary;
4. connect the paid offer to the Greene Leadership System commercial source of truth;
5. launch free 7-Day sampler → 30-Day membership conversion path;
6. add new themed journeys as recurring monthly releases;
7. extend to family and institutional licensing packages without exposing private reflection content.

## Release gate

Do not merge this foundation to production merely because the preview builds successfully.

Product-owner review should confirm:

- all 30 day titles, prompts, strengths, Hero Moves, and affirmations feel canon-consistent;
- the experience is usable on iPhone and desktop;
- progress/navigation are understandable;
- private/local storage language is accurate;
- studio-audio pending status is unmistakable;
- no paid-access claim appears before commerce is configured;
- the 7-Day free experience remains intact.

Only after that review should the branch move toward a production release decision.
