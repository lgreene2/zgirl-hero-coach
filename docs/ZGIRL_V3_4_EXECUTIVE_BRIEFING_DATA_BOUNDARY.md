# Z-Girl v3.4 Executive Briefing Data Boundary

## Allowed executive reporting data
v3.4 may use institution-level administrative and commercial data such as:
- institution name/type/status
- institutional license term/status/capacity
- seat utilization
- adult facilitator/trainer credential administrative status and renewal exposure in aggregate
- agreement/release workflow status
- institutional implementation handoff status
- partner opportunity stage, target date, and planning value
- executive portfolio health, strategic priority, expansion readiness, owner, next action, and review date
- aggregate briefing and exception counts

## Prohibited data
The briefing system must not collect, copy, infer, summarize, or expose:
- participant private-reflection text
- youth/student/athlete/family case-management records
- diagnoses or treatment records
- therapy or counseling notes
- safeguarding narratives or incident details
- clinical records
- clergy/spiritual-direction records
- sports-medicine records
- participant behavior scoring
- credential assessment answers, scores, practicum notes, or evaluator detail in executive reporting

## Forecast semantics
Renewal and expansion forecasts are administrative decision-support signals. They are not predictions of participant behavior or outcomes.

A renewal-risk label describes institutional timing, workflow readiness, capacity, and administrative exposure only.

An expansion signal describes institutional readiness/capacity/opportunity only. It does not authorize expansion.

## Delivery boundary
The automated system may prepare a draft addressed to a configured executive recipient. It does not autonomously send email. The delivery queue is restricted to authorized operators using the Credential Operations session.

## Authority boundary
No executive briefing artifact can replace the authoritative controls for:
- executed agreements
- approval gates
- credential issuance/renewal/status
- institutional license activation/renewal/expansion/status
- implementation release/handoff
- payment confirmation

## Reporting audience
Packets may be used internally and, after deliberate human review, for boards, funders, strategic partners, or institutional leadership. Operators are responsible for confirming that any externally distributed packet is appropriate for the recipient and does not disclose confidential institutional administrative information beyond the intended scope.
