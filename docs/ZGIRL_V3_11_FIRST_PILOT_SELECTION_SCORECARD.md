# Z-Girl v3.11 — First Real Pilot Selection Scorecard

Use this scorecard before creating the first real Z-Girl pilot. Availability alone is not enough.

Score each dimension **0–3**:

- 0 — absent / unknown / material concern
- 1 — weak / substantial work required
- 2 — workable / manageable conditions
- 3 — strong / pilot-friendly conditions

## Core fit — 24 points

1. **Accessible decision maker** — can make or directly sponsor the pilot decision.
2. **Defined participant group** — clear cohort, setting and approximate capacity.
3. **Manageable first-pilot size** — enough participants to learn, small enough to support well.
4. **Structured-feedback willingness** — administrator/facilitator willing to participate in midpoint and closeout feedback.
5. **Credible use case** — Z-Girl directly addresses an identified reflection/character/mindset/self-development implementation need.
6. **Realistic implementation access** — schedule, devices/materials, facilitator time and operational access are realistic.
7. **Safety/privacy readiness** — institution can support escalation, participant choice and privacy boundaries.
8. **Facilitator capacity** — at least one appropriate adult can reliably support implementation.

## Evidence / growth value — 12 points

9. **Case-study/reference potential** — institution has credibility and is open to a governed permission discussion after results exist.
10. **Renewal potential** — a successful pilot has a realistic annual-license or repeat-cycle path.
11. **Expansion potential** — additional cohorts/sites/departments/teams/profiles are plausible.
12. **GLS relationship potential** — complementary Greene Leadership System offerings could create a broader institutional relationship without forcing the fit.

## Risk deductions

Subtract points for material risks:

- **−3** unclear safeguarding/escalation owner
- **−3** expectation of private-reflection access or participant surveillance
- **−3** expectation that Z-Girl function as therapy/diagnosis/clinical care
- **−2** unstable implementation schedule or leadership sponsorship
- **−2** participant group too large for a first evidence-building pilot
- **−2** no meaningful feedback access
- **−2** unresolved accessibility/accommodation barriers
- **−2** unclear contracting/commercial/nonprofit boundary

## Recommended selection thresholds

- **30–36:** preferred first-pilot candidate
- **24–29:** viable with named conditions/blockers
- **18–23:** do not activate yet; improve readiness first
- **below 18:** not appropriate for the first evidence-building pilot

A candidate with a high score may still be rejected if a non-negotiable privacy, participant-safety, authority, or IP/commercial boundary is unresolved.

## Tie-breakers

When two candidates score similarly, prefer the institution with:

1. easier access to the actual decision maker;
2. smaller/more controllable first cohort;
3. stronger facilitator commitment;
4. cleaner implementation calendar;
5. better structured-feedback access;
6. stronger renewal/expansion path;
7. higher likelihood of a credible permission-based reference after the pilot.

## Decision record

For the selected institution record:

- score and date
- evaluator
- top strengths
- unresolved conditions
- why this institution was chosen over alternatives
- expected evidence value
- risk controls
- proposed pilot profile/capacity/window
- next GLS action

Do not create a public case-study or testimonial expectation before evidence exists and explicit permission is granted.
