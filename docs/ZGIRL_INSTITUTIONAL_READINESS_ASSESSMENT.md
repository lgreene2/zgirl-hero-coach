# Z-Girl Hero Within — Institutional Readiness Assessment

Use this assessment before confirming a Z-Girl institutional pilot launch.

## Assessment outcome

Select one final status:

- **READY** — required launch conditions are in place.
- **READY WITH CONDITIONS** — launch may proceed only after listed conditions are completed by the stated date.
- **NOT YET READY** — material implementation, safeguarding, staffing, accessibility, policy, technology, or communication gaps should be resolved before launch.

## Critical launch blockers

A pilot should normally be **NOT YET READY** if any of the following remain unresolved:

- no accountable implementation owner;
- no local safeguarding/emergency route;
- expectation that staff will receive private participant reflections as routine reporting;
- facilitators have not been oriented to privacy and role boundaries;
- required institutional approvals or family communications are missing;
- known accessibility barriers have no reasonable implementation plan;
- the institution intends to market Z-Girl as clinical treatment, crisis intervention, or another service it is not;
- material contract/procurement conditions prohibit launch.
