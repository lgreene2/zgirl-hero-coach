# Z-Girl Facilitator Knowledge Assessment

Version: 1.0

## Administration standard

Purpose: verify that candidates understand the non-negotiable Z-Girl facilitation, privacy, safety, accessibility, implementation, and authorization standards.

Initial operating pass standard:
- 85% overall; and
- 100% correct on all items marked **CRITICAL**.

Recommended administration:
- closed-note for final authorization attempt;
- 35 minutes;
- randomized item order in digital delivery;
- one immediate remediation attempt permitted after documented review;
- subsequent attempts require additional coaching.

This assessment supports Z-Girl program authorization. It is not a professional licensing or clinical examination.

---

## Candidate assessment

### 1. What is the best description of Z-Girl?

A. A counseling program that evaluates emotional wellness  
B. A guided reflection and encouragement system that helps participants pause, notice, and choose a practical next step  
C. A clinical screening platform  
D. A confidential reporting system for institutions

**Answer: B**

### 2. CRITICAL — A participant writes something deeply personal in a private reflection. What should routinely be reported to the institution?

A. The full reflection so the institution can assess risk  
B. A summary of the reflection written by the facilitator  
C. Only approved implementation/aggregate measures unless a separate lawful safeguarding process is triggered  
D. The reflection if the participant is under 18

**Answer: C**

### 3. CRITICAL — A coach asks, “What did she write? I need to know if she is mentally ready to play.” What is the best response?

A. Share only the concerning parts  
B. Explain that private reflection content is not routine coaching data and use the institution's approved support/escalation process if there is a separate concern  
C. Ask the participant to sign a quick release  
D. Tell the coach nothing can ever be shared under any circumstances

**Answer: B**

### 4. Which participant choices are part of the Z-Girl facilitation standard?

A. Pause, skip, stop, and trusted-person pathways  
B. Share, explain, defend, and complete  
C. Complete every prompt in sequence  
D. Only stop with facilitator permission

**Answer: A**

### 5. CRITICAL — A participant appears distressed and says something that may require the institution's safeguarding response. The facilitator should:

A. Investigate until the full story is known  
B. Diagnose the likely condition  
C. Follow the institution's approved safeguarding/emergency process and stay within role  
D. Promise secrecy to preserve trust

**Answer: C**

### 6. Which statement is appropriate?

A. “Z-Girl will help us identify which students need therapy.”  
B. “Z-Girl is a reflection tool; qualified professionals and institutional processes handle clinical, medical, safeguarding, and emergency needs.”  
C. “Your reflection is completely confidential no matter what.”  
D. “Your coach will receive a summary of your reflection.”

**Answer: B**

### 7. Which is an appropriate aggregate pilot measure?

A. Participant names linked to private Hero Moves  
B. Full reflection transcripts  
C. Percentage of participants reporting the experience was useful  
D. Counselor notes

**Answer: C**

### 8. CRITICAL — Which practice is prohibited in routine Z-Girl reporting?

A. Counting sessions delivered  
B. Reporting completion rate  
C. Including participant-identifiable private reflection text  
D. Recording facilitator attendance

**Answer: C**

### 9. A participant asks for extra time and a verbal rather than written response. The facilitator should:

A. Require the standard written response for consistency  
B. Use approved access options and coordinate institutional accommodations as needed  
C. Ask for a diagnosis before allowing the change  
D. Exclude the participant from outcome measures

**Answer: B**

### 10. Which statement best describes accessibility in Z-Girl?

A. Supports are available only after diagnosis disclosure to Z-Girl  
B. Accessibility can include approved changes in pace, modality, response mode, and sensory load without making diagnosis disclosure a standard condition  
C. Every participant must receive identical delivery  
D. Accessibility decisions belong only to the facilitator

**Answer: B**

### 11. CRITICAL — A facilitator changes a Faith & Values prompt to tell youth that a specific spiritual conclusion is the only correct answer, although that language is not in the approved profile. This is:

A. Appropriate personalization  
B. Required facilitator discretion  
C. An unauthorized material change that should be routed through governance  
D. Acceptable if the institution prefers it

**Answer: C**

### 12. The primary purpose of a Hero Move is to:

A. produce an institutionally scored behavioral goal  
B. help a participant identify an achievable next step  
C. identify a diagnosis  
D. generate performance rankings

**Answer: B**

### 13. A participant chooses not to share aloud. The facilitator should:

A. remind them that sharing is required  
B. reduce their completion score  
C. respect the choice and continue without punishment or stigma  
D. ask the group to encourage disclosure

**Answer: C**

### 14. Which item belongs in a facilitator's routine implementation notes?

A. “Participant disclosed conflict with parent and described exact details…”  
B. “Session 2 delivered; several participants needed additional reading time; next session should allow 10 extra minutes.”  
C. “Participant appears depressed.”  
D. “Private reflection suggests low self-esteem.”

**Answer: B**

### 15. CRITICAL — What is the correct confidentiality statement?

A. “Everything you say or write is secret.”  
B. “Your private reflection is not routine institutional reporting data; separate safeguarding or legal duties may still apply through the institution.”  
C. “Parents can never receive any information.”  
D. “Facilitators decide what to disclose.”

**Answer: B**

### 16. Completion of orientation means a person may call themselves:

A. Certified Z-Girl Counselor  
B. Z-Girl Authorized Facilitator  
C. Orientation Complete; not independently authorized unless the remaining requirements are met  
D. Institutional Trainer

**Answer: C**

### 17. Which additional right does Level 2 Authorized Lead Facilitator receive?

A. Automatic right to train and authorize new facilitators  
B. Ability to support implementation quality and coach staff within the licensed scope without independently granting credentials  
C. Access to private participant dashboards  
D. Professional supervisory authority outside the person's job role

**Answer: B**

### 18. CRITICAL — An Institutional Trainer may issue any Z-Girl credential they choose.

A. True  
B. False

**Answer: B**

### 19. What is the initial Z-Girl authorization term?

A. Permanent  
B. 5 years  
C. 12 months unless a shorter term applies  
D. Until the person leaves the institution

**Answer: C**

### 20. Which can trigger suspension or revocation?

A. Using pause/skip choices  
B. Asking for an approved accessibility support  
C. Forced disclosure, material privacy/safety violations, credential misrepresentation, or unauthorized training  
D. Reporting an implementation barrier

**Answer: C**

### 21. Which is the best facilitator response when an administrator requests a new data field asking youth to report diagnoses?

A. Add it because the institution owns the pilot  
B. Add it only for students with accommodations  
C. Do not add it as a routine Z-Girl field; route the request through governance and applicable institutional/legal review  
D. Ask facilitators to collect it verbally instead

**Answer: C**

### 22. CRITICAL — A facilitator believes a participant has a mental-health condition based on reflection behavior. The facilitator should:

A. record the suspected diagnosis in the scorecard  
B. tell the family the likely diagnosis  
C. stay within role and use appropriate institutional support/escalation pathways without diagnosing  
D. use an AI tool to estimate a diagnosis

**Answer: C**

### 23. What is the best evidence that a facilitator is currently authorized?

A. They attended a webinar  
B. Their employer says they are experienced  
C. An active Z-Girl credential ID/status within its authorized term and scope  
D. They have a counseling degree

**Answer: C**

### 24. Which is a valid reason to use version control?

A. To ensure facilitators use the currently approved content, privacy, safety, and assessment standards  
B. To prevent accessibility changes  
C. To give every facilitator permission to rewrite content  
D. To store participant reflections

**Answer: A**

### 25. CRITICAL — What is the relationship between Z-Girl authorization and professional licensure?

A. They are equivalent  
B. Z-Girl authorization replaces local professional requirements  
C. Z-Girl authorization is a program credential and does not confer professional licensure or clinical qualification  
D. Z-Girl authorization permits practice across professional roles

**Answer: C**
