# Z-Girl Professional Support Gateway v1

Status: CANDIDATE / NOT AN ACTIVE CLINICAL NETWORK

## Product doctrine

Z-Girl is the AI-adaptive reflection, preparation, and human-support layer. It is not a therapist, counselor, medical provider, crisis service, diagnosis engine, treatment recommender, or clinical record system.

Core flow:

Reflect → identify need → choose human support → prepare → participant-controlled handoff → optional follow-up.

## v1 shipped scope

- Professional Support Gateway public surface.
- Existing-professional pathway into the current participant-controlled support brief.
- Institutional pathway into existing Z-Girl partner access.
- Provider-network interest surface for licensed professionals and qualified organizations.
- Explicit clinical independence, privacy, safety, and pre-activation disclosures.
- No public provider directory, matching, booking, telehealth session, clinical messaging, diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, or clinical-record feature.

## Activation gates before live provider discovery / virtual counseling

1. Legal/regulatory review for target launch jurisdictions.
2. Provider credential and standing verification process.
3. Jurisdiction-aware provider eligibility and participant location controls.
4. Minor/guardian consent and assent policy by use case and jurisdiction.
5. Privacy architecture and health-data determination, including BAAs where applicable.
6. Clinical-record boundary and data-retention policy.
7. Mandated-reporting and safeguarding workflow.
8. Crisis / emergency escalation protocol and provider responsibility matrix.
9. Accessibility and language-access review.
10. Provider terms, participant disclosures, complaint/removal process, and quality governance.
11. Commercial model review: no unapproved fee-splitting, clinical steering, pay-for-diagnosis, or compensation that compromises professional independence.
12. Owner approval before activating live provider discovery, referrals, booking, clinical handoff, or monetization.

## Data boundary

Participant reflections remain private by default. A professional does not receive reflection content merely because the participant selects a professional-support pathway. The participant chooses what to include in a support brief and reviews it before sharing. A provider's clinical record remains the provider's responsibility under the provider's own professional and legal obligations.

## Commercial candidates (not activated)

- Professional workspace subscription for practices / organizations.
- Institutional integration license.
- Provider directory / network participation model only after legal and ethical review.
- Sponsored access where sponsor receives no participant-level reflection or clinical data.

Ordinary affiliate/reseller economics must not be applied automatically to clinical referrals.
