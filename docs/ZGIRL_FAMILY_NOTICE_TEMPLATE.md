# Z-Girl Hero Within — Family / Guardian Notice Template

Use this template as a customizable starting point when family, guardian, caregiver, or participant communication is appropriate or required. The institution should adapt the notice to the approved Z-Girl pathway, audience, local policy, legal requirements, consent/permission process, schedule, and support structure.

> This template is not legal advice and does not determine whether consent, permission, assent, notice, opt-out, or another process is legally required. The institution remains responsible for that determination.

---

## Family / Guardian Notice

**Subject: [Organization Name] Z-Girl Hero Within Reflection Pilot**

Dear Families / Guardians,

[Organization Name] plans to offer a limited **Z-Girl Hero Within** reflection experience for [group / grade / team / program] from [start date] through [end date].

Z-Girl is a guided reflection and encouragement system designed to help participants pause, think about what they are experiencing, recognize strengths and support, consider constructive next steps, and identify an achievable **Hero Move**.

### What the experience is designed to support

Depending on the approved program pathway, participants may reflect on topics such as:

- confidence and self-awareness;
- leadership and character;
- values and decision-making;
- strengths and support systems;
- athlete mindset or team development;
- practical next steps and personal growth.

### What Z-Girl is not

Z-Girl is **not** therapy, medical care, clinical diagnosis, crisis intervention, emergency services, clergy, spiritual direction, sports medicine, or a substitute for a qualified professional.

If a participant needs support beyond the reflection activity, [Organization Name] will continue to use its existing support, safeguarding, emergency, counseling, medical, pastoral, or other appropriate procedures.

### Participant choice

Participants will be reminded that they may:

- pause;
- skip a prompt;
- stop the activity;
- ask for a trusted person;
- keep private reflection responses private rather than reading them aloud.

### Privacy approach

The Z-Girl institutional model is designed so that **private participant reflection text is not routine institutional reporting data**.

[Organization Name] will use approved implementation and aggregate feedback measures to understand whether the pilot was accessible, useful, respectful, and practical to implement. These measures may include items such as overall participation opportunity, completion, usefulness, confidence, return/reuse, accessibility experience, safety/respect experience, and facilitator implementation quality.

Private journal-style reflection text is not part of the standard aggregate pilot scorecard.

### What families should know about local policies

[Organization Name] remains responsible for its own policies and legal obligations related to student/youth records, safeguarding, emergencies, consent/permission, accessibility, technology, mandated reporting, and other local requirements.

If a participant voluntarily tells a staff member something that triggers the staff member’s legal or institutional responsibilities, the staff member must follow the organization’s normal procedures. Z-Girl does not override those duties.

### Accessibility and participation support

We want the activity to be reasonably accessible. If your child / participant needs practical support related to reading, language, pacing, sensory needs, technology, communication, or another access issue, please contact [local accessibility/support contact].

A participant should not be required to submit a diagnosis to Z-Girl simply to request reasonable implementation support.
