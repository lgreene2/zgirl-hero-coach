# Z-Girl Train-the-Trainer & Facilitator Authorization

## Scale delivery without lowering the standard

Z-Girl's Train-the-Trainer pathway helps approved schools, youth programs, congregations, athlete organizations, and multi-site partners prepare internal facilitators while preserving participant privacy, role boundaries, implementation quality, accessibility, and version control.

## Best fit

Organizations that:
- have completed or are preparing for an institutional Z-Girl pilot/license;
- want more internal delivery capacity;
- need a consistent facilitator standard across teams/sites;
- want repeatable onboarding instead of one-off staff training;
- value privacy-first implementation and governed quality assurance.

## Authorization ladder

**Level 0 — Orientation Complete**  
Introductory preparation; not independent authorization.

**Level 1 — Z-Girl Authorized Facilitator**  
Complete core training, pass knowledge assessment and practicum, and receive a time-limited program credential.

**Level 2 — Z-Girl Authorized Lead Facilitator**  
Demonstrate successful implementation evidence and lead quality/coaching inside the licensed scope.

**Level 3 — Institutional Trainer Authorization**  
Complete trainer preparation, teach-back, scoring calibration, and credential-administration requirements under an active institutional trainer license.

## What makes the credential defensible

- 8-module core curriculum
- 25-item knowledge assessment
- 100% required on critical privacy/safety items
- 16-domain observed practicum rubric
- critical-fail rules
- authorization decision record and credential ID
- 12-month term
- renewal and version-update requirements
- remediation, suspension, revocation, and appeal process
- credential registry/version control
- institutional trainer license boundaries

## Core institutional benefit

One trained person is not the product. The product is a **repeatable quality system** that lets an organization grow delivery capacity while Z-Girl retains the standards that protect participants and the IP.

## Privacy-first boundary

The credential evaluates facilitator competence. It does **not** make participant private reflection text part of facilitator assessment or routine credential reporting.

Institutions do not receive a new private-reflection dashboard through Train-the-Trainer.

## Credential boundary

Z-Girl authorization is a program credential. It is not:
- professional licensure;
- academic accreditation;
- government certification;
- clinical qualification;
- counseling, medical, pastoral, sports-medicine, or safeguarding licensure.
