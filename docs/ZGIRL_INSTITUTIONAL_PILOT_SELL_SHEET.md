# Z-Girl Hero Within — Institutional Pilot Sell Sheet

## Reflection without surveillance.

**Z-Girl Hero Within** is a guided reflection system for schools, youth programs, faith communities, athlete teams, and other youth-serving organizations. It helps participants pause, name what is happening, identify strengths and support, and choose one achievable next step — a **Hero Move**.

The institutional model is intentionally privacy-first: organizations support the reflection process without receiving access to private participant entries.

---

## Who it is for

- Schools and districts
- After-school and mentoring programs
- Youth-serving nonprofits
- Congregations and faith communities
- Athletic teams, clubs, and leagues
- Camps and enrichment programs
- Approved specialized implementation partners

---

## The problem it solves

Youth-serving organizations often want practical ways to support reflection, confidence, character, self-awareness, decision-making, and trusted-adult connection without creating another surveillance system, counseling record, or high-burden curriculum.

Z-Girl provides a structured, facilitator-supported reflection experience with clear implementation boundaries, family communication, accessibility considerations, and a light institutional data footprint.

---

## What a pilot includes

A typical institutional pilot includes:

1. **Readiness review** — audience, setting, timeline, staffing, technology, accessibility, safeguarding, and implementation ownership.
2. **Facilitator / coach orientation** — privacy, safety, accessibility, implementation flow, and when to involve real-world support.
3. **Participant reflection sequence** — a focused four-session, four-week, or 30-day experience depending on the approved profile.
4. **Family communication resources** — plain-language notice and implementation information where appropriate.
5. **Facilitator resources** — session guidance, implementation checklist, opening / closing language, and support boundaries.
6. **Aggregate outcome scorecard** — participation, completion, usefulness, confidence, action, accessibility, and safety measures.
7. **Closeout review** — findings, implementation lessons, and a decision to stop, revise, renew, expand, or license.

## Institutional pathways

### Z-Girl EDU
For schools and youth programs. A privacy-first 30-day facilitated reflection experience with staff orientation, family communication, accessibility support, aggregate outcome measures, and a structured closeout decision.

### Faith & Values
For congregations and faith communities. Uses governed faith / values profiles, facilitator guidance, family-facing resources, and boundaries between spiritual reflection and professional care.

### Athlete Edition
For teams and leagues. A four-week mindset and character pilot with coach orientation, athlete reflection resources, family communication, accessibility guidance, and a findings summary.

### Specialized Design Partner
For approved organizations with specialized accessibility, content, integration, or implementation needs that require controlled co-design.

## What institutions receive

- Structured implementation plan
- Facilitator / coach orientation
- Approved implementation resources
- Family-facing communication materials where applicable
- Accessibility and safeguarding guidance
- Approved content profile
- Aggregate pilot scorecard
- Pilot findings / closeout summary
- Recommended next-step pathway
- Priority consideration for annual licensing and train-the-trainer expansion

---

## What institutions do **not** receive

- Private participant reflections
- Student or athlete journals
- A youth-surveillance dashboard
- Clinical or diagnostic scoring
- Counseling records
- Individual mental-health profiles
- Permission to represent Z-Girl as therapy, medical care, or emergency support

**The reflection belongs to the participant.**

---

## Suggested pilot outcomes

Institutional pilots can review approved aggregate measures such as:

- sessions offered
- participation
- completion
- usefulness
- confidence / self-efficacy proxy
- return / reuse
- identified Hero Move / action
- accessibility experience
- safety / respect experience
- facilitator implementation quality

Private reflection text is excluded from institutional reporting.

---

## Pilot-to-license pathway

**Fit review → Pilot → Aggregate learning → Decision → Annual license → Train-the-Trainer / Expansion**

Z-Girl is designed to start with a focused learning cycle rather than force organizations into a large platform commitment before fit is proven.

## Commercial and governance boundary

Z-Girl commercial products and institutional licenses are commercial transactions and must remain separate from charitable donations.

Paid checkout remains gated until the approved merchant of record is fully active. Institutional pilots may continue through inquiry, proposal, reservation, and invoicing workflows without changing that boundary.
