# Z-Girl Institutional Trainer License Framework

Version: 1.0 business framework for agreement drafting

## Purpose

Define the commercial and governance structure under which an approved institution may use Z-Girl training materials and an authorized Institutional Trainer to prepare facilitator candidates inside the licensed scope.

This is a business framework for contracting and counsel review. It is not legal advice and is not itself an executed license.

## License grant

Subject to the executed agreement, provider grants the institution a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable right during the term to:
- access current approved facilitator-training materials;
- deliver training to approved internal candidates through active Level 3 Institutional Trainer(s);
- administer approved candidate practice and assessments as authorized;
- submit candidate evidence/recommendations through the governed credential process;
- use approved Z-Girl trainer/facilitator marks only as expressly permitted.

## Rights not granted

Unless separately authorized in writing, the institution may not:
- sublicense Z-Girl IP;
- sell independent Z-Girl training to outside organizations;
- create derivative certification programs;
- issue “Certified Z-Girl” credentials;
- change passing standards;
- remove critical privacy/safety assessment items;
- materially modify governed training content;
- use private participant reflections as training or credential evidence;
- represent Z-Girl authorization as professional licensure or clinical qualification;
- credential people outside the approved process;
- train trainers beyond the licensed trainer tier.

## Authorized audience

Agreement should identify:
- institution/network/sites;
- staff role types eligible for training;
- maximum or contracted candidate seats where applicable;
- approved profiles (Core, EDU, Faith & Values, Athlete, other approved profile);
- geographic or organizational scope;
- whether external contractors are included.

## Trainer requirements

Each trainer must:
- maintain active Level 3 status;
- use current training version;
- complete annual calibration;
- follow assessment/practicum standards;
- maintain candidate evidence;
- report material credential concerns;
- avoid representing recommendation authority as unrestricted credential-issuance authority.

Loss, suspension, or lapse of trainer authorization may suspend the institution's right to deliver training until an active approved trainer is available.

## Candidate authorization process

Default model:
1. trainer verifies candidate eligibility;
2. candidate completes training;
3. candidate passes knowledge assessment;
4. candidate passes practicum;
5. trainer submits required evidence and recommendation;
6. Z-Girl credential authority validates the file and issues/denies authorization;
7. credential registry is updated.

An agreement may delegate limited administrative steps, but critical standards and credential governance remain controlled.

## Quality assurance

Provider may require:
- periodic trainer calibration;
- anonymized/administrative training metrics;
- sample candidate-file review;
- observation of training delivery;
- corrective-action plans;
- version-update completion;
- audit of credential records relevant to the license.

Quality review should not require routine access to participant private reflections.

## Data boundary

Trainer/credential administration may process:
- candidate name/contact;
- role/institution;
- training completion;
- assessment scores;
- observation rubric scores;
- remediation records;
- credential status/term;
- profile/version scope.

Routine credential administration should not process participant private reflection text.

## Safeguarding and employment boundary

Institution retains responsibility for:
- staff eligibility/employment decisions;
- background checks where applicable;
- mandated-reporting/safeguarding obligations;
- emergency procedures;
- professional licensure requirements;
- supervision under local law/policy.

Z-Girl authorization does not replace these duties.

## Intellectual property

Agreement should define:
- ownership of Z-Girl content, marks, curriculum, rubrics, assessments, and derivative training materials;
- limited permitted copying/use;
- restrictions on extraction/republication;
- confidentiality of answer keys and trainer-only assessment materials;
- return/destruction/access termination on expiration where appropriate.

## Credential marks

Only active credential holders may use approved authorization marks. Institution must stop displaying active-status claims for suspended, revoked, lapsed, or expired credentials once notified.

## Term and renewal

Recommended structure:
- 12-month institutional trainer license;
- renewal aligned with trainer authorization and annual license where possible;
- no automatic perpetual training rights;
- version updates incorporated into renewal/continuation conditions.

## Commercial structure

Possible pricing components, to be set by approved commercial policy:
- annual institutional trainer license;
- trainer seat/authorization fee;
- facilitator candidate seat fee;
- initial training/onboarding fee;
- renewal/reauthorization fee;
- multi-site/network tier;
- premium calibration/observation support.

Do not publish or collect payment against unapproved pricing or merchant-of-record configuration.

## Suspension / termination

Provider should retain rights to restrict or terminate training rights for:
- material privacy/safety violation;
- repeated scoring/credential integrity failure;
- unauthorized sublicensing or derivative certification;
- IP misuse;
- nonpayment where applicable;
- trainer authorization lapse without replacement;
- material breach not cured within applicable period.

## Agreement exhibits

Recommended exhibits:

C. Candidate/seat allocation  
D. Approved profiles and versions

## Future certification boundary

The institutional trainer license supports **Z-Girl program authorization**. It does not authorize the institution to create or market a professional certification unless a future, separately governed certification license expressly permits it.
