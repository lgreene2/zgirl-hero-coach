# Z-Girl Open v2.0.2 Release Notes

## Sound Cue Refinement

- Removes the response-complete chime.
- Removes send, save, and reflection-launch chimes.
- Retains one optional, very low-volume cue only when spoken voice playback begins.
- Renames the control to **Sound cues** and defaults it to **Off**.
- Resets legacy v2.0.1 sound preferences to Off while preserving voice, language, speed, pitch, and preferred-device-voice settings.
- Keeps spoken voice output fully independent from sound cues.
- Updates the service-worker cache version so installed copies receive the patch.

## Unchanged

- Natural feminine voice selection and per-language voice preferences remain intact.
- English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, and German speech options remain intact.
- Safety controls, reflection content, transcripts, and PWA behavior are otherwise unchanged.
