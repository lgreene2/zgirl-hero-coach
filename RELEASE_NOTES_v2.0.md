# Z-Girl Open v2.0 Release Notes

## Added

- Public v2.0 product gateway and edition architecture
- Six-step Hero Within private reflection flow
- Youth, adult, family, and guided-group prompt paths
- Interactive 7-Day Hero Within Journey with device-local progress
- Larger-text and low-stimulation reflection controls
- Plain-language Privacy & Data Guide
- Accessibility page and WCAG 2.2 AA target
- Print, save-PDF, copy, and download options

## Upgraded

- AI Coach now offers youth, adult, and supporter editions
- AI behavioral and safety instructions are controlled on the server
- Deterministic high-risk responses do not require the AI provider
- Crisis language includes U.S. 988 and 911 guidance
- Public data-flow language distinguishes private reflection from AI chat
- PWA name, theme, metadata, version, and cache updated to v2.0
- Seasonal Holiday Hero Mode removed from the active product

## Preserved

- Z-Girl character identity and The 4 Lessons connection
- Voice input and spoken-response controls
- Browser-local Hero Moments and conversation controls
- School and district pilot routes and files, now labeled as v1.1 archive materials
